A broader direction

My current work in speech recognition and turn-taking motivates a broader interest in computational models of human cognition: how language is processed incrementally, and how interaction unfolds under uncertainty.

Emerging interests include NeuroAI, Human–AI interaction, Multimodal intelligence, Computational pragmatics. These are directions for future study, alongside my established work in speech and conversational AI.
